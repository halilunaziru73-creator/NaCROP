# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-08-27)
- Initial public release of NaCROP: renamed and extended from the earlier
  Zaria Crop ET and Irrigation DSS pipeline, with a new 4-step crop-growth
  simulation stage (canopy cover, transpiration, biomass, yield), interactive
  parameter prompts, and a root-zone-depth water-balance bug fix.
